# Risk analysis

## Categories to check
- **Financial**: customer or revenue concentration, margin pressure, leverage, cash needs, valuation risk.
- **Regulatory**: export controls, antitrust, tax, data and AI rules, geopolitical exposure.
- **Competitive**: rivals, customers building in-house alternatives, pricing pressure.
- **Technology**: product transitions, execution or supply-chain dependencies, obsolescence.
- **Business model**: demand cyclicality, dependence on a few products or partners, sustainability of growth.

## For each risk
- **Description**: what could happen, in one sentence.
- **Evidence**: cited source (title, URL, date); say if it is the company's own disclosure.
- **Likelihood**: low, medium or high, with a one-line reason.
- **Impact**: low, medium or high, with a one-line reason.
- **Watch for**: a signal that would show the risk is rising.

## Method
- Rank by likelihood and impact together; put the highest first.
- Prefer risk factors in filings plus recent news; check dates.
- Separate documented risks from your own inference and label each.

## State uncertainty
- Ratings are judgments: say how confident you are and what is unknown.
- Flag gaps or conflicting sources instead of smoothing them over.
